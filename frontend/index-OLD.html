AliExpress Authentication — 3 steps, one-time setup

1
Get your AliExpress OAuth login URL
Click below — the backend will return your unique login URL.
2
Open the URL → Login → Authorize
Click Open — log in with your AliExpress account and click Authorize.
AliExpress will redirect to your /callback URL. The backend automatically exchanges the code for an access token and saves it to the database.
—
3
Verify token is saved
After authorizing in your browser, come back here and click Verify Token. Once verified, you can fetch and import products.
